# Luma challenge

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, required by the supplied challenge.

## Product purpose

Reproduce the supplied Luma course landing page as a recruitment exercise. The page
introduces practical courses, shows social proof and offers a local contact interaction.
It is not a live learning platform and does not create accounts or send messages.

## Capabilities and constraints

All visible desktop/mobile sections and imagery are binding. Backend/CMS are excluded.
Course content and claims are supplied demonstration content from Figma, not claims
about the candidate. Ambiguous interactions must be documented rather than invented
as real services. Source must be public; the English README explains the process and AI.

## Evidence on hand

Supplied Figma, original PDF in the candidate's Downloads, full frame exports under
the local ignored `.artifacts/design/` directory and Figma imagery under `public/images/`.

## Accessibility

Semantic landmarks, keyboard-friendly controls, image alternatives, metadata and
reduced-motion support are challenge requirements.
